Cashflow analyzer - analyze your spendings for free

- What does it do?
  add a budget with all of your spendings and then you could go to analyze page where you can see highest spending category, highest spending and how much, avg spending per month, and line graph and piechart for spendings.

- After analyzing spendings, does it provide a solution?
  you can take the budget and all of the spendings and apply the 50-30-20 financal strategy by selecting your needs, and wants, and savings and see if you adhereing to that strategy.
